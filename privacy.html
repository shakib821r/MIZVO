Privacy Policy

Draft for the MIZVO early version. Last updated 2026-11-07. This text has not been reviewed by a lawyer and may change.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address, password (kept by our sign-in provider in protected form, never in readable form), full name, and account dates.
  • Profile details you add: username, status and profile photo.
  • Messages: not available yet. This page will be updated before messaging launches.

Who can see what

Your name, username, status and photo can be seen by other signed-in MIZVO users. Your email address is not shown to them.

How we use it

To let you sign in, show your profile, and send account emails such as email verification and password reset.

Services we use

  • Supabase for sign-in and the database.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting this website.
  • Gmail to send account emails.
  • Google Fonts to load the fonts of this site.

MIZVO does not currently sell personal data or show advertising.

Your choices

You can change your profile details in the app once the profile screens are available. To ask for your data to be corrected or your account to be deleted, email us at the address below.

Age

MIZVO is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Questions: mizvo.official@gmail.com
